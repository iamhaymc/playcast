          How to play

          1. Every action is a chord. Press moves to pick a target, then runes to pick a spell. The spell fires on the last rune.
          2. Targets. You start on yourself. jumps to the foe row, back to yours, walk along a row.
          3. Runes. Each spell has a chord of 2 to 4 runes. Chords change every match, so study the book before the fight.
          4. Offense spells need a foe. Defense spells need an ally or yourself. A wrong chord, target, mana or cooldown fizzles and stalls you briefly.
          5. Read the field. Life, mana, shields, casts and effects of every mage show above them. Interrupt casts, cleanse allies, finish foes.
          6. The surge. Healing fades over time. When the rift surges, everyone takes rising damage until a team falls.
