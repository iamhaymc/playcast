          How to play

          1. Draft. Each match deals a new book. Before the fight, type a spell's chord (or tap it) to put it in your kit. Only your kit can be cast.
          2. Every action is a chord. Moves aim, runes pick the spell, and it fires on the last rune. Type fast: a quick chord casts up to 40% faster.
          3. Aim sticks. jumps to the foe row, back to yours (again to snap to you), walk a row. The aim stays between chords. Ally spells aimed at a foe land on you.
          4. Metamagic. Before the last rune, then a rune shapes the spell: Quicken (instant), Twin (also the mage beside), Empower (40% stronger, slower), Extend (effects last 50% longer). Each costs more mana, and they stack.
          5. Ward. raises a ward for 0.3s. A hostile spell that lands on it turns back on its caster, and the ward is ready again in 1s. Miss and it rests 5s.
          6. Reactions. Hit a foe's bane with the right school: fire melts frost, storm shatters frost, frost steams fire, nature spreads fire, arcane overloads storm, holy purifies shadow, shadow eclipses holy.
          7. Flow. Clean casts, parries and reactions build up to 5 flow: faster casts, cheaper spells. A fizzle or an interrupt breaks it.
          8. Stuns fade. Repeat stuns and silences from foes land at ½, then ¼, then not at all. After a stun ends you get a moment of grace.
          9. The rift shifts. Every 45s and when the surge begins, a quarter of the chords change. Healing fades over time, and the surge hurts everyone until a team falls.
          10. Fallen, not out. A fallen mage drifts as a wisp with three tiny spells. calls your aim for the team.
